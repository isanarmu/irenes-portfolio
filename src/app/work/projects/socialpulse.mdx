## The project

SocialPulse is a SaaS platform focused on digital emotional intelligence. I contribute as a Junior Full Stack Developer, improving the website and working within an existing product codebase with feedback from the team.

## Hero and product positioning

I worked on the landing experience, navigation, calls to action and responsive behaviour. The hero introduces the product around real-time analysis of public conversations and keeps the main actions visible without overwhelming the page.

![SocialPulse hero](https://image.thum.io/get/width/1200/crop/900/noanimate/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fsocialpulse.es%2F%23hero)

## How it works

The product flow explains how SocialPulse moves from public conversations to useful emotional intelligence. I refined the layout, spacing and responsive presentation so the steps remain easy to scan on desktop and mobile.

![How SocialPulse works](https://image.thum.io/get/width/1200/crop/900/noanimate/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fsocialpulse.es%2F%23how-it-works)

## Who it is for

The audience section presents the product for creators and SMEs, agencies and research-oriented users. My work focused on keeping the cards, typography and visual hierarchy consistent across breakpoints.

![Who SocialPulse is for](https://image.thum.io/get/width/1200/crop/900/noanimate/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fsocialpulse.es%2F%23model)

## Lead capture and contact flow

I implemented and refined the contact modal with separate flows for investors, beta users and general enquiries. This included validation, saving submissions in Firestore and connecting confirmation and notification emails through Resend.

The modal was also adapted for mobile so its controls, selects and scrolling remain usable on smaller screens.

## Login and navigation

I worked on the navigation behaviour and the login entry point as part of the wider responsive interface. The navbar changes subtly on scroll while keeping the main product sections and contact action accessible.

![SocialPulse login](https://image.thum.io/get/width/1200/crop/900/noanimate/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fsocialpulse.es%2Flogin)

## Product intelligence and final experience

I contributed to sections presenting capabilities such as the Emotion Index, narrative detection and audience comparison, as well as the final calls to action and footer. The goal was to make a data-heavy product feel clear and visually consistent rather than overly technical.

![SocialPulse product section](https://image.thum.io/get/width/1200/crop/900/noanimate/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fsocialpulse.es%2F%23product)

## Technologies

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
- **Data and integrations:** Firebase Admin, Firestore and Resend.
- **Workflow and deployment:** Git, GitHub and Vercel.
- **Supporting work:** responsive UI, SEO metadata, sitemap, robots configuration and provisional legal pages.

## What this experience taught me

SocialPulse gave me experience maintaining and extending someone else's code, translating team feedback into concrete interface improvements and connecting frontend forms to backend services. My contribution focuses on the website and these integrations; the wider platform is a team product.
