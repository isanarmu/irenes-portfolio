## The project

SocialPulse is a SaaS platform focused on digital emotional intelligence. I contribute as a Junior Full Stack Developer, improving the website and working within an existing product codebase with feedback from the team.

## My contribution

### Responsive interface and product pages

I worked on the navigation, product sections, calls to action and mobile layouts. This included refining spacing, typography, alignment and interactions so that the interface stays consistent across screen sizes.

### Lead capture and email workflows

I implemented and refined a contact modal with separate flows for investors, beta users and general enquiries. My work included form validation, storing submissions in Firestore, and connecting email notifications and confirmations through Resend.

### SEO and supporting pages

I contributed page titles and descriptions, Open Graph and social sharing metadata, the sitemap and robots configuration. I also integrated provisional legal content and improved navigation between pages and sections.

### Working in a real codebase

I used Git and GitHub to manage changes, responded to team feedback, checked production builds and worked on Vercel configuration for Firebase and Resend integrations.

## Technologies

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
- **Data and integrations:** Firebase Admin, Firestore and Resend.
- **Workflow and deployment:** Git, GitHub and Vercel.

## What this experience taught me

SocialPulse gave me experience maintaining and extending someone else's code, translating feedback into specific interface improvements, and connecting frontend forms to backend services. My contribution focuses on the website and these integrations; the wider platform is a team product.
